# Keyword Placement Guidelines

> **Status: Task-derived draft — pending editorial approval.**
> This is the new rules document required by MM03-01. It controls placement of the supplied terms; it does not research, verify or replace them.

## Primary keyword

The exact supplied primary keyword must appear in:

- The meta title.
- The H1.
- The first 100 words of the body.
- At least one H2.

## Related keywords

- Use the supplied related keywords as final inputs.
- Distribute them across relevant sections rather than grouping them into one paragraph.
- Use a related keyword only where it fits the section's meaning.
- Do not introduce competitor-derived terms or revise the supplied list.

## Stuffing prevention

- Do not repeat a keyword where it makes the sentence unnatural or redundant.
- Prefer clear, reader-first language over repeated exact-match phrasing after the required placements are satisfied.
- Flag concentrated repetition within a short passage for review.
- Warn when the same adjective appears at least four times and at a rate of four or more uses per 1,000 prose words.
- Exclude headings, quotations/callouts, required keyword phrases and likely product/proper names from adjective counting. The warning is editorial guidance, never a blocker.

## Automated checks

The deterministic checker must report:

- Missing primary keyword in the meta title.
- Missing primary keyword in the H1.
- Missing primary keyword in the first 100 words.
- Missing primary keyword from all H2 headings.
- Related keywords absent from meaningful body sections.
- Repetition above an approved density or concentration threshold.

## Approved concentration threshold

The operator approved the repeated-adjective warning threshold above for local implementation. Keyword density remains governed by reader-first placement and the existing concentrated exact-phrase warning; no unsupported overall keyword percentage is invented.
